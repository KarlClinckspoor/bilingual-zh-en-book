# HSK 3.0 level 2

Ten short stories, each with one small problem and its solution and a little real cultural content.
Every word and character is in the HSK 3.0 list at level 1 or 2, so the only footnotes are for names.
The plan and grammar map are in [plans/hsk2-stories.md](../../plans/hsk2-stories.md).

Every story has 100 % of running words (names excluded) at or below HSK 2, counting a word as known if
it is in the word list or all its characters are in the character list at that level
(`hsk_report.py --target 2`). The PDFs are built with `--annotate-above 2 --annotate-kinds N`.
