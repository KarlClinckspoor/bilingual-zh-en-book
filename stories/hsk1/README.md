# HSK 3.0 level 1

Ten very short texts about everyday life, in simple sentences with a lot of natural repetition. Every
word and character is in the HSK 3.0 list at level 1, so the only footnotes are for names. The plan
and grammar map are in [plans/hsk1-stories.md](../../plans/hsk1-stories.md).

Every story has 100 % of running words (names excluded) at HSK 1, counting a word as known if it is in
the level-1 word list or all its characters are in the level-1 character list
(`hsk_report.py --target 1`). The PDFs are built with `--annotate-above 1 --annotate-kinds N`.
