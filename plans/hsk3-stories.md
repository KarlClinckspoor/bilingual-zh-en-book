# Plan: 10 stories for HSK 3.0 Level 3 (stories 42–51)

Complete short stories with a plot turn: four retold legends and fables, and six modern stories
with real cultural or social content.

## Targets
See `plans/writing-brief-hsk1-2-3-6.md`. In short:
- 1,300–1,900 hanzi, 3–5 `@h2` sections, dialogue in “……”.
- ≥ 92 % of running words at or below HSK 3. Most of the rest are names (N), terms (J) or idioms (I).
- Footnotes for names, terms, idioms and words above HSK 3. Build: `make_book.py … --annotate-above 3`.

## The stories

### 42 · 守株待兔和别的故事 — *Waiting by the Tree, and Other Fables*
- **Content:** four classical fables, each ending with the idiom it gave Chinese: 守株待兔, 画蛇添足,
  亡羊补牢, 井底之蛙. Idioms are `I` tokens with gloss.
- **Plot (frame):** A teacher, Mr Zhou (周老师), tells one fable a day for four days to a class that
  keeps making the mistake of the day before. On the last day, a student tells the fable herself.
- **Sections:** 第一个故事：守株待兔 · 第二个故事：画蛇添足 · 第三个故事：亡羊补牢 · 第四个故事：井底之蛙
  (shorter titles are fine).

### 43 · 司马光砸缸 — *Sima Guang Breaks the Jar*
- **Content:** two famous childhood stories from Chinese history: Sima Guang (司马光, Song dynasty)
  breaking the water jar to save a friend, and Kong Rong (孔融, Han dynasty) giving away the bigger
  pears. What they show about Chinese ideas of quick thinking and politeness. Mention briefly that
  Sima Guang grew up to write a great history book (资治通鉴, J/N with gloss).
- **Plot (frame):** A boy, Lele (乐乐), won't share with his little sister. His grandfather tells him
  both stories. Lele objects to the Kong Rong one ("why should the small one take the small pear?").
  The two talk it over, and Lele finds his own way to share.
- **Sections:** 不想给妹妹 · 大水缸 · 孔融让梨 · 乐乐的办法

### 44 · 白蛇传 — *The Legend of the White Snake*
- **Content:** the legend: the white snake Bai Suzhen (白素贞) and Xiao Qing (小青) become women, meet
  Xu Xian (许仙) on the Broken Bridge at West Lake (西湖, 断桥) in the rain, the umbrella, the monk
  Fahai (法海), the Duanwu wine, Leifeng Pagoda (雷峰塔). Keep the most common modern version.
- **Plot (frame):** A young couple on holiday in Hangzhou are caught in the rain on the Broken Bridge
  with one umbrella. A tour guide tells the legend. At the end they see Leifeng Pagoda, and the story
  links back to them lightly.
- **Sections:** 西湖的雨 · 一把伞 · 法海 · 雷峰塔

### 45 · 送错的快递 — *The Wrong Parcel*
- **Content:** life in a big apartment block in a Chinese city: parcels (快递), delivery people, phone
  apps, neighbours who never meet.
- **Plot:** A parcel for Room 1602 goes to Room 1206 by mistake. Ms Huang (黄阿姨) in 1206, a retired
  teacher, takes it up herself and meets Zhao Jie (赵杰), a young programmer who never leaves the
  flat. It happens again, and again (the delivery man is new). Soon the block has a chat group, and
  the old and young help each other.
- **Sections:** 1206 还是 1602？ · 楼上的年轻人 · 又送错了 · 一个新的群

### 46 · 共享单车 — *Shared Bikes*
- **Content:** shared bikes in Chinese cities: scan a code, ride, pay with the phone. The problem of
  bikes piled everywhere around 2017–2018, and how cities deal with it (parking areas, rules).
- **Plot:** University student Xu Na (许娜) loves shared bikes, but the bikes outside her school are a
  mess. She and two friends make a small plan for the area around the school gate and show it to the
  school. Not everything works, but it gets better.
- **Sections:** 扫一扫 · 门口的车山 · 我们的办法 · 一个月以后

### 47 · 长江里的微笑 — *The Smile in the Yangtze*
- **Content:** the Yangtze finless porpoise (长江江豚), which looks as if it smiles. Overfishing,
  pollution and boats; the ten-year fishing ban on the Yangtze (began 1 January 2020, J); former
  fishermen now work as river guards. Fact-check: keep numbers vague (大约, 据说) or skip them.
- **Plot:** Old fisherman Yu (老余) has fished the river for forty years. He sees fewer fish every
  year and has not seen a porpoise for a long time. When the ban comes, he is angry and lost. He
  becomes a river guard. One morning, a porpoise appears beside his boat.
- **Sections:** 打了四十年鱼 · 不能打鱼了 · 新工作 · 江里的微笑

### 48 · 风筝 — *The Kite Maker of Weifang*
- **Content:** kite-making in Weifang (潍坊), Shandong: bamboo frames, paper or silk, painting; the
  international kite festival. Making something by hand versus buying it.
- **Plot:** Xiaoxue (小雪) is sent to her grandfather in Weifang for the summer and is bored. He
  makes kites. She wants to buy one; he says only a kite you make yourself flies well. They make a
  swallow kite together, and her first one crashes. At the kite festival it flies.
- **Sections:** 无聊的夏天 · 爷爷的风筝 · 第一次 · 在天上

### 49 · 去朋友家做客 — *Dinner at a Friend's Home*
- **Content:** Chinese etiquette as a guest: bringing a gift, taking off shoes, the host serving food
  onto your plate, saying no politely three times, leaving a little food, the fight to pay or to see
  the guest out.
- **Plot:** Exchange student Lucas (卢卡斯) is invited to dinner at his classmate Zhang Hao's
  (张浩) home. He has read about the rules, but they don't all work as he expected. He finishes every
  dish, so the host keeps bringing more. Zhang Hao explains afterwards. Lucas invites the family to
  his place and teaches them his own country's customs.
- **Sections:** 带什么礼物？ · 吃不完的菜 · 我来！ · 到我家来

### 50 · 十二生肖 — *The Twelve Animals*
- **Content:** the folk legend of the zodiac race: the Jade Emperor (玉皇大帝, N), the rat riding on the
  ox, why the cat is not in the list. The order of the twelve animals; people ask each other's animal
  instead of age. Animal words above level 3 (老鼠, 老虎, 兔子, 猴子, 蛇) are fine with footnotes.
- **Plot (frame):** On New Year's Eve, two children argue whose animal is better. Their grandmother
  tells the race. At the end, the family works out everyone's animal, and the grandmother's is the
  same as the youngest child's (twelve years × 6).
- **Sections:** 你属什么？ · 一场比赛 · 猫和老鼠 · 一家人的生肖

### 51 · 机器人服务员 — *The Robot Waiter*
- **Content:** robots in restaurants and hotels in China, ordering with QR codes, what machines can and
  can't do.
- **Plot:** Grandpa Sun (孙爷爷) refuses to eat at a new restaurant where a robot brings the food. His
  grandson drags him there. The robot is funny and fast. Then it gets stuck and spills soup, and the
  only human waiter is too busy. Grandpa helps her carry the dishes. He leaves saying the restaurant
  needs more people, not fewer robots, and comes back the next week anyway.
- **Sections:** 我不去 · 会说话的机器人 · 汤 · 下个星期
