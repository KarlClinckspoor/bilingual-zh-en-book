# Plan: 10 stories for HSK 3.0 Level 6 (stories 52–61)

Longer literary stories and narrative biographies. Compared with HSK 5 they use more voices, time
jumps, irony and open endings, and some classical lines (footnoted, translated in the English).
Every story has a person who has to decide what really matters to them.

## Targets
See `plans/writing-brief-hsk1-2-3-6.md`. In short:
- 3,800–4,800 hanzi, 6–9 `@h2` sections, dialogue in “……”.
- ≥ 90 % of running words at or below HSK 6. Most of the rest are names (N), terms (J) or idioms (I).
- About 50–80 HSK 6 words per story, each used at least twice where natural.
- Footnotes for names, terms, idioms and words above HSK 6. Build: `make_book.py … --annotate-above 6`.

## The stories

### 52 · 苏东坡在黄州 — *Su Dongpo in Exile*
- **Content:** Su Shi (苏轼, 1037–1101). After the Crow Terrace poetry case (乌台诗案, 1079) he is
  sent to Huangzhou (黄州) in 1080 as a minor official without power. He farms a slope east of the town
  (东坡) and takes the name 东坡居士; the pork recipe (东坡肉, his 《猪肉颂》 is the legend's source); the
  two 赤壁赋 (1082) and 《念奴娇·赤壁怀古》; the poem 《定风波》 ("一蓑烟雨任平生"). How disaster turns
  into his best work.
- **Plot:** Told partly through a fictional young servant or a local farmer who helps him clear the
  land. Opens in prison with Su Shi expecting death, then the boat to Huangzhou, poverty, the land, the
  rain on the road (定风波), the night boat under the cliffs, and his leaving in 1084.
- **Sections:** 牢里的诗 · 到黄州 · 东坡 · 一锅肉 · 雨中的路 · 赤壁之夜 · 离开
- **Fact-check:** dates, which poems, the claim that 赤壁 at Huangzhou is probably not the battle site
  (he knew it, "人道是"). Mark legends as legends.

### 53 · 敦煌的修画人 — *The Mural Restorers of Dunhuang*
- **Content:** the Mogao Caves (莫高窟): murals from the 4th to the 14th century, damage from sand,
  salt and humidity (and visitors' breath), the slow restoration work, the "Digital Dunhuang" project
  that photographs every cave so the murals can be seen online.
- **Plot:** A young art-school graduate, Lin Qiu (林秋, fictional), joins the restoration team and
  expects art. She gets months of practice on old walls and a strict master, Teacher Ma (马老师),
  who came in the 1980s. A stage in which they work on one damaged flying apsara (飞天) for a whole
  season. The master is retiring; she has to decide whether to stay in the desert.
- **Sections:** 沙漠里的火车 · 第一面墙 · 马老师 · 盐和沙 · 一个飞天 · 照相机里的洞窟 · 留下
- **Fact-check:** dates of the caves, how restoration works, Digital Dunhuang (launched online
  around 2016). Keep real living people out of the plot.

### 54 · 塞罕坝 — *The Forest That Was Planted*
- **Content:** Saihanba (塞罕坝), Hebei. Once forest, by the mid-20th century a sandy wasteland
  north of Beijing. From 1962 a state forest farm (机械林场) with a few hundred young people, most
  in their early twenties, planted trees in extreme cold; early failures; the forest now one of the
  largest planted forests in the world; the UN Champions of the Earth award (2017).
- **Plot:** Three generations of one fictional family: the grandmother who came in 1962 at 19, her
  son who looked after the fire watchtower (望火楼) for years, and the granddaughter, a forestry data
  scientist with drones. The granddaughter reads her grandmother's old diary.
- **Sections:** 一九六二年的风 · 第一年，树都死了 · 马蹄坑 (the 1964 successful planting, J) · 望火楼 ·
  奶奶的日记 · 无人机 · 一片林子
- **Fact-check:** dates and numbers with 大约 / 据说. The 1964 马蹄坑 planting can be named.

### 55 · 棋馆的最后一局 — *The Last Game at the Go Club*
- **Content:** Go (围棋): its age, its place in Chinese culture (琴棋书画), and what happened after
  AlphaGo beat Lee Sedol in 2016 and Ke Jie in 2017 (mention the facts only; the characters are fictional).
  Players now study with AI. Does a game still mean something if a machine is better?
- **Plot:** Old Teacher Qian (钱老师) runs a small Go club in a Hangzhou lane. Students leave to train
  with AI apps. His best former student, now a pro, comes back to tell him the club should close or
  "go online". They play one last game, the pro using moves learned from AI. Qian loses, and then
  shows him something the machine did not teach: why he played as he did.
- **Sections:** 巷子里的棋馆 · 二〇一六年 · 走了的学生 · 回来的人 · 最后一局 · 棋子的声音

### 56 · 三十分钟 — *Thirty Minutes*
- **Content:** food-delivery riders (外卖骑手) in big cities: the app's timer and route algorithm,
  ratings, rain and traffic, the debate around 2020 about the system putting riders at risk, and
  how platforms changed some rules. Keep it human and fair, not a lecture.
- **Plot:** One rainy day of Zhou Hai (周海), a rider in Shenzhen who came from a village in Hunan.
  Each section is one order. One customer, a young office worker who always writes "please hurry",
  meets him at the door at night. Both are tired, both are working for someone else's clock. A
  small change in each.
- **Sections:** 早上六点 · 第一单 · 雨 · 红灯 · 一个差评 · 十二楼的女孩 · 晚上十一点

### 57 · 爷爷的方言 — *Grandpa's Dialect*
- **Content:** Chinese dialects (方言): Putonghua and its spread, dialects losing speakers among the
  young, projects that record dialects (e.g. the national 语保工程, started 2015, J), and how a dialect
  carries old words, songs and humour.
- **Plot:** University student Chen Yu (陈雨) in Shanghai can understand but not speak her family's
  Wenzhou dialect (温州话), famous for being hard to understand. Her grandfather, who is getting
  forgetful, lights up when he speaks it. She starts recording him for a class project: words, nursery
  rhymes, stories. She ends by answering him, badly, in his dialect.
- **Sections:** 听得懂，不会说 · 爷爷忘了什么 · 录音机 · 一首童谣 · 消失的词 · 第一句温州话

### 58 · 一千六百年前的药方 — *A 1,600-Year-Old Recipe*
- **Content:** Tu Youyou (屠呦呦, b. 1930) and artemisinin (青蒿素). Project 523 (from 1967); she led a
  team from 1969; after many failures, she found in Ge Hong's (葛洪) 4th-century 《肘后备急方》 the line
  "青蒿一握，以水二升渍，绞取汁，尽服之", realised that heat might destroy the active substance, tried
  low-temperature extraction with ether, and the team's sample 191 worked (1971). She and colleagues
  tested it on themselves. Nobel Prize in Physiology or Medicine, 2015. Malaria today. **No medical
  advice.** She is a living person: only well-documented facts, respectful tone.
- **Plot:** Narrative biography, framed by a young pharmacy student in 2015 watching the Nobel news
  with her grandmother, who remembers malaria in her village.
- **Sections:** 二〇一五年十月 · 疟疾 · 五二三 · 两千个药方 · 一句古文 · 第一百九十一号 · 自己先试 · 屋子里的灯
- **Fact-check:** dates, 191, the quote. Use 据说 / 大约 for numbers of recipes.

### 59 · 庄子的梦 — *Zhuangzi's Dreams*
- **Content:** Zhuangzi (庄子, 4th century BC) and four of his stories: the butterfly dream (庄周梦蝶),
  the joy of fish (濠梁之辩 with Huizi 惠子), the useless tree (无用之用), Cook Ding (庖丁解牛). Short
  classical lines, footnoted and translated.
- **Plot (frame):** Gu Fan (顾帆), a burnt-out programmer in Beijing, finds a cheap illustrated
  Zhuangzi at a bookstall the night he decides to quit. Each section, one story meets one part of his
  week: a dream, an argument with a colleague, being "useless" after a bad review, the craft of doing
  one thing well. The ending stays open: he does not quit, or he does, and either is fine.
- **Sections:** 凌晨两点 · 蝴蝶 · 鱼的快乐 · 没用的树 · 庖丁 · 醒来

### 60 · 运河人家 — *The Canal Family*
- **Content:** the Grand Canal (京杭大运河): the Sui dynasty canals (from about 605), the Yuan route
  north to Beijing, grain transport (漕运) and Huai'an (淮安) as its centre in the Ming and Qing, the
  decline after railways and the sea route, UNESCO World Heritage 2014, the canal today.
- **Plot:** One fictional family in Huai'an over centuries, told in non-linear sections: a Ming
  boatman, a Qing grain official's clerk, a 1950s ferry worker, and today's granddaughter, a guide on a
  canal tourist boat who finds the family name carved on an old stone.
- **Sections:** 石头上的名字 (today) · 隋朝的河 · 明朝的船夫 · 漕运 · 火车来了 · 一九五八年的渡口 · 申遗 · 河还在流
- **Fact-check:** dates with 大约. Keep the family fictional.

### 61 · 陪伴 — *Company*
- **Content:** ageing China and "empty nest" parents (空巢老人); children working far away; elderly
  care; AI companion robots and the questions they raise.
- **Plot:** Her children in Shenzhen buy their widowed mother in a small city in Anhui a talking
  companion robot. She resents it, then talks to it every day, more than she talks to them. When her
  son comes home for the Spring Festival, he finds she has taught the robot her husband's jokes. Who
  is the robot really for? The ending stays open.
- **Sections:** 一个箱子 · 它会说话 · 晚饭时间 · 老伴的笑话 · 视频里的儿子 · 回家过年 · 谁陪谁
